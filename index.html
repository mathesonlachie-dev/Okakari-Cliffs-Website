Our Story

About Okakari Cliffs

Who We Are

A Place Where
Nature Takes Centre Stage

Okakari Cliffs is nestled along the spectacular Auckland coastline — a remarkable venue where the raw power of the sea and the quiet grandeur of the cliffs create an unmatched natural canvas.

We opened our gates with a singular vision: to offer couples and event hosts a setting so breathtaking, so deeply connected to the New Zealand landscape, that no decoration could rival it. The cliffs speak for themselves.

Our team is passionate about crafting experiences that honour both the landscape and the people who gather within it. Whether you're planning an intimate ceremony for thirty or a grand reception for three hundred, we bring the same care and artisanal attention to every detail.

What We Believe

Our Values

01

Authentic Beauty

We believe the most powerful setting is one that is unquestionably real. No manufactured backdrop — just the honest, raw beauty of the New Zealand coast.

02

Personalised Care

Every couple and every event is unique. We work closely with you to understand your vision and shape every element of the day around what matters most.

03

Environmental Respect

The cliffs are a living landscape. We are committed to operating in a way that honours and preserves this coastal environment for generations to come.

Venue Details

What the Venue Offers

Location

Auckland Region, New Zealand. Set along a dramatic stretch of coastline, accessible via private road with ample on-site parking.

Capacity

We accommodate intimate gatherings of 20 guests through to grand celebrations of up to 300, with flexible configurations for any event style.

Availability

Available year-round, with peak season October through March. Private hire is exclusive — your date is entirely yours.

Spaces

Clifftop ceremony lawn, covered terrace, reception pavilion, bridal suite, and multiple photography locations across the property.
